The Ambat Family — A Living Legacy

Our family heritage traces back to the early St. Thomas Christian community of Kerala, rooted in a 1st-century Christian tradition and enriched by centuries of migration, faith, and resilience. From our origins in Niranam and Nilackal to our settlement in Bharananganam and the formation of the Ambat branches, our story is one of faith, community, and continuity. We preserve this history so future generations may know where we came from — and where we are going.
